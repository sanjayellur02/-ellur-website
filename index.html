Frequently asked questions

Do I need internet to use it?

No. Ellur works 100% offline. Internet is only needed for initial installation and optional updates.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Your data never leaves your computer. Backups are encrypted with AES-256 military-grade encryption. No cloud, no servers, no data collection.

Can I import from Tally?

Yes. Export your masters from Tally as XML, and import them in one click. Ledgers, stock items, parties, and opening balances transfer automatically.

What databases are supported?

PostgreSQL (recommended). We provide a one-click installer guide. It's free and open source.

What OS is supported?

Windows 10+, macOS 12+, and Ubuntu/Debian Linux. Installers are provided for all three.

Can I try before I buy?

Yes. The app includes a 30-day free trial with full features. No credit card required.

What if I need help?

Email support for all plans. WhatsApp and phone support for Business and Enterprise. We also provide installation assistance.
